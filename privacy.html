Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Overview

Domino Scorekeeper does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

Data stored on your device

Player names, scores, and round history are stored locally on your device only using iOS standard local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data we do not collect

Domino Scorekeeper does not use analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or any third-party tracking. No account or registration is required. We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any other personal information.

Children

Domino Scorekeeper does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
