Domino Scorekeeper

Simple scorekeeping for Mexican Train dominoes. 2–4 players, round-by-round history, share results with your group.

How to use

Tap New Game, enter player names, and score each round as you play. Tap a player's row to enter their tile totals or a direct score. When the game is over, tap End Game to see final standings.

Frequently asked questions

How many players can I track?
2 to 4 players per game.

Can I pause and come back to a game?
Yes — unfinished games are saved automatically and can be continued from the home screen.

What does "low score wins" mean?
In Mexican Train, the player with the fewest total pips remaining wins each round. Domino Scorekeeper adds up scores accordingly.

Is my data backed up?
Game history is stored locally on your device. It is not backed up to the cloud.
